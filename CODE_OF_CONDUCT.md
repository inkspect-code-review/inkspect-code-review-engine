# Community expectations

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Critique ideas and code rather than
people. Help newcomers and assume good intent when asking for clarification.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized content, and deliberate
sharing of private information or credentials are not acceptable. These rules
apply to this project's issues, pull requests and other project-managed spaces.

Report concerns privately to privacy@inkspect.si, with links and enough context
for maintainers to investigate. Do not disclose another person's private details
in an issue. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict access
when needed, and will consider context and repeated behavior.
